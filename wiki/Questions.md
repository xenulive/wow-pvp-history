# Questions

Short answers to what people ask most. Each one links to the page with the full story.

## Do I have to do anything for it to record?

No. Once it's installed, it records every match on its own, whether or not you ever open the window.
See [Getting started](Getting-Started).

## Can other players see my notes or reps?

No. Your notes, reps, tags and comments are only ever shown to you. The addon never sends anything to a
chat channel or to another player.

## Does it send my history anywhere?

No. Your history stays in a file on your computer. It only leaves if you copy it out yourself, such as
with **Export** or `/pvph export csv`. See [Moving your history](Settings-and-Storage#moving-your-history-to-another-character-or-computer).

## Does it work on all my characters?

Yes. Every character on your account shares one history. The dropdown at the top right of the window
shows one character at a time, or **All Characters**. See
[Showing one character](Match-History-Window#showing-one-character).

## Will I lose my history if I update or reinstall the addon?

No. Your history isn't kept in the addon's folder, so replacing or deleting that folder leaves it alone.
See [Updating the addon](Settings-and-Storage#updating-the-addon).

## How do I move my history to a new computer?

Use **Export** on the old computer and **Import** on the new one, on the **Data and backups** page of the settings. Or
copy the saved files across with the game closed. See
[Where your history is kept](Settings-and-Storage#where-your-history-is-kept).

## A match I played isn't there. Why?

Usually one of three things: you left before the end (look under the **Other** tab), it was a duel
the game didn't print a result for, or the game crashed before it saved. See
[A match is missing](Troubleshooting#a-match-is-missing).

## How do I stop the pop-ups and chat lines?

Each one has its own switch:

| What you see | Turn it off with |
| --- | --- |
| A line in chat after every match | **Chat line after a match**, or `/pvph postgame off` |
| The match window opening by itself after a match | **Open the match window**, or `/pvph aftermatch off` |
| Names across your screen at the start of a match | `/pvph announce off` |
| A line in chat when your group changes | `/pvph alert off` |
| A mark and a chat line when someone you wrote about appears | **A mark on their nameplate**, or `/pvph sight off` |
| The addon's lines on player tooltips | `/pvph tooltip off` |

**A mark on their nameplate** is on the **Notes and players** page of the
[settings](Settings-and-Storage), and the other named switches are on its **Alerts** page. See [Alerts](Alerts)
for what each one does.

## Why doesn't it show who killed whom, or interrupts?

The game doesn't let any addon see those during a match. See
[What it cannot record](What-It-Cannot-Record).

## How do I start over?

**Erase** under **ERASE EVERYTHING STORED**, at the bottom of **Data and backups** in the settings. It asks you to
confirm, and keeps a copy you can bring back. See [Starting over](Settings-and-Storage#starting-over).

## Where's the full list of commands?

On the [Commands](Commands) page, or type `/pvph help` in game.
