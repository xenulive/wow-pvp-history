# PvPHistory

A World of Warcraft addon that remembers your PvP matches. Arenas, Solo Shuffle, battlegrounds, Blitz,
duels and World PvP are all saved, along with everyone who played in them, so you can look back at how
a match went, how your rating moved, and who you've played with and against.

The game doesn't keep this for you: the scoreboard disappears when you leave the match. The addon saves
your history in the game's own addon files on your computer, and there's nothing else to install or
sign up for.

These pages describe version **0.70.16**. Each version, with what it changed and its download, is listed on the [releases page](https://github.com/xenulive/wow-pvp-history/releases), newest first.

## Getting started

New to the addon? [Getting started](Getting-Started) goes step by step from installing it to your first
saved match. In short:

1. Install it from the [releases page](https://github.com/xenulive/wow-pvp-history/releases): download the zip under the newest release, close the game, and unzip it into the `_retail_\Interface\AddOns` folder inside your World of Warcraft folder, so that a `WowPvpHistory` folder sits there. Or install it through the CurseForge app from [its CurseForge page](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/wow-pvp-history) or the Wago app from [its Wago page](https://addons.wago.io/addons/wow-pvp-history), which keep it up to date, or download it from [its WoWInterface page](https://www.wowinterface.com/downloads/info27266-PVPHistory.html).
2. Log in. The addon starts recording on its own, with nothing to set up.
3. Play a match, then open your history with any of these:
   - type `/pvph`
   - click the minimap button
   - pick it from the addon menu beside the minimap
   - set a key for **Open the match history** under **Key Bindings**, in the **PvPHistory**
     section
