# Words used here

The words these pages use, in plain terms. Most are the game's own.

## Kinds of match

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Arena | A small team fight, two against two (2v2) or three against three (3v3). |
| Skirmish | An arena match with no rating on it. |
| Solo Shuffle | A rated arena mode for six players queuing alone. It plays six rounds, and the teams are shuffled every round so you play with and against everyone once. |
| Round | One fight inside a Solo Shuffle. A match has six. |
| Shuffle Brawl | Solo Shuffle with no rating. |
| Battleground | A large team fight over objectives such as flags, bases or carts. |
| Blitz | Battleground Blitz, a rated battleground mode you can queue for alone. Blitz Brawl is the same with no rating. |
| Duel | A one-on-one fight you ask another player for in the open world. |
| World PvP | Fighting other players in the open world with War Mode on. |
| Outing | One stretch of World PvP, from your first kill or death until 15 minutes pass with neither. The **World PvP** tab lists outings where other tabs list matches. |
| War Mode | The game's switch for fighting other players in the open world. With it off, there's no World PvP to save. |
| Rated, unrated | A rated match moves your rating. An unrated one doesn't. |

## Ratings

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Rating | The number the game gives you for each rated mode. It goes up when you win and down when you lose. |
| MMR | Matchmaking rating: a hidden number the game uses to pick who you play against. It can be higher or lower than your rating. **MMR GAP** is how far apart the two are. |
| Tier | The named rating band you're in. Each tier has its own color, and your rating is drawn in it. |
| Peak | The highest rating you've reached. |
| Season | A stretch of time the game's ratings run for. Ratings reset when a new season starts. |

## Players and teams

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Spec | A class's specialization, such as Frost Mage or Holy Paladin. |
| Comp | Short for team composition: the specs on one team, such as two damage dealers and a healer. |
| Rep | The rating you give a player yourself, from **Bad** to **Strong**. Only you see it. |
| Tag | A short label you put on a player, such as `healer` or `duo`. |
| Note | Anything you write about a player or a match. Only you see it. |

## In the addon's windows

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Session | Matches played within an hour of each other. The list groups your matches by session. |
| Match number | The number the addon gives each saved match, such as `#12`. Type `/pvph 12` to see that match in chat. |
| Win rate | How many of your matches you won, as a percentage. |
| Decided match | A match with a winner. A draw isn't decided, so it's left out of your win rate. |
| Even | A match nobody won, such as a Solo Shuffle where you won three rounds of six. |
| Left early | A match you left before it finished. It's saved under the **Other** tab with less in it. |
| DISP | Dispels: how many harmful effects a player removed, as the game's own damage meter counted them. |

## Typing commands

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Slash command | Something you type in the chat box starting with `/`, such as `/pvph`. Press Enter to run it. |
| `/reload` | Reloads the game's interface without logging out. The game also saves addon data when you do this. |
| Saved file | The file the game keeps your history in, on your computer. See [Where your history is kept](Settings-and-Storage#where-your-history-is-kept). |
